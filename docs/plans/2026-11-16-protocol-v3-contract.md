## Basis and scope

The following choices are implementation conventions adopted in this session; they do not reconstruct the missing conversation.

## Status and compatibility

- New templates declare `Protocol version: 3`.
- `### Needs you now` belongs under the existing `## Coordination` section.
- The legacy standalone `## Needs you now` section remains readable and valid.
- An absent or empty attention section remains valid; multiple attention sections are rejected as ambiguous.
- Each attention row retains `Task | Question | Resume when`; the default maximum is five rows.
- Only unique tasks whose normalized state is `Completed` contribute to `completed_count`.
- More than ten completed tasks produce an advisory warning; warnings leave `valid` and the exit status unchanged.
- `Cancelled` tasks do not contribute to this count. Archiving preserves evidence and history pointers; it is not automatic deletion.
- Task IDs, legal states, history, revision checks, and active contracts retain their existing meaning.

## Shared limits and parsing

`scripts/validation_limits.py` owns the defaults: five attention rows, ten completed tasks before warning, 2 MiB per knowledge file, twenty LOG body lines, and two hundred summary characters.
The existing optional state-spec attention limit override remains compatible.
Knowledge limits retain their previous values and warning/error semantics.

`scripts/markdown_table.py` owns shared status section and cell parsing.
Section bodies end at the next heading and exclude fenced examples.
Both status validation and rendering consume this parser.
Only configured task/blocked heading levels supply authoritative tables; nested historical tables cannot replace current task state.

## Methodology and durable knowledge

`grill-with-docs` supplies interview and terminology methods.
Task Commander retains output paths, role ownership, and confirmed decision rules.
`CONTEXT.md` holds terms and domain relations; `decision-log.md` holds confirmed decisions.

`knowledge/ROADMAP.md` is optional direction across sessions.
`project-status.md` remains the task-state source; ROADMAP does not duplicate live task tables.

## Delivery and checks

Changes include the validator, renderer, knowledge validator, shared helpers, status template/spec, protocol references, README, regression tests, and CI syntax checks.
The latest Commander scope and user-decision confirmation rules are preserved.
Acceptance requires new/legacy status compatibility, warning boundary coverage, knowledge-limit regression coverage, and the complete unittest suite.

Temporary logs, scratch status files, and generated bytecode are removed after checks.
Reusable regression tests and protocol documents are deliverables and remain in the repository.
After implementation, the user authorized cache cleanup, commit and push. Remote model evaluation remains outside this integration.
